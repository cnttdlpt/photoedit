# Awesome DESIGN.md — Benchmark Design System Library

A comprehensive repository of production-grade `DESIGN.md` files analyzed and extracted from top digital products, luxury brands, and developer tools.

## When to Use This Skill

- When designing or redesigning a product interface and looking for proven, benchmark design systems in the same industry or aesthetic archetype.
- When drafting a new `DESIGN.md` for a project or verifying token completeness (colors, typography, corner radii, elevation, spacing, component semantics).
- When asked to adopt or reference a specific design aesthetic (e.g. "make it feel like Linear", "like Apple", "like Clay", "like Stripe", or "like Arc").

## How to Apply a Benchmark DESIGN.md

1. **Locate the benchmark file:**
   Read `design-md/<product-name>/DESIGN.md` in this skill directory to study its typography scales, color roles (primary, paper, panel, ink, muted, stage), elevation rules, and component specs.

2. **Extract & Adapt:**
   Map the benchmark's tokens and spatial principles to the current project's functional needs while respecting brand identity and platform conventions.

3. **Validate:**
   Ensure contrast ratios meet WCAG AA (>= 4.5:1), touch targets are >= 44px, and dark mode roles invert surface and ink consistently.
