# Open Design — Collaborative Design Workspace & Templates

Open Design provides a comprehensive repository of battle-tested design templates, layout engines, and specialized agent skills.

## Core Templates & Workflows (`skills/`)

### 1. Presentation & Pitch Decks
- **`deck-swiss-international`** — High-precision typography, bold asymmetrical grid systems, and rationalist Swiss layout principles.
- **`deck-guizang-editorial`** — Editorial luxury presentation layouts with high-contrast serif headlines and generous whitespace.
- **`deck-open-slide-canvas`** — Infinite canvas presentation layouts for product roadmaps and strategic vision decks.

### 2. Editorial & Publishing Layouts
- **`article-magazine`** — Multi-column editorial magazine layouts with pull quotes, drop caps, and author signatures.
- **`doc-kami-parchment`** — Tactile paper and parchment-textured document styling for research papers and whitepapers.
- **`field-notes-editorial-template`** — Technical field notebook styling with numbered sections, monospaced metadata, and archival accents.

### 3. Conversion & Growth UI
- **`paywall-upgrade-cro`** — High-converting pricing tiers, paywalls, feature comparison tables, and checkout flows.
- **`mockup-device-3d`** — Photorealistic 3D device frames (iPhone, MacBook, iPad, Studio Display) for showcasing screenshots.
- **`login-flow`** — Frictionless authentication screens with biometric prompts and social sign-in.

### 4. Interactive 3D & Data Visualization
- **`threejs`** — Procedural 3D scene creation, orbit camera controls, and physical material shaders.
- **`d3-visualization`** — Custom interactive data graphics, hierarchical chord diagrams, and real-time telemetry charts.
- **`motion-lab`** — Physics-based spring animations and choreography playgrounds.

## How to Apply Open Design Templates

1. Identify the target deliverable (e.g. pitch deck, editorial landing, paywall, 3D mockup).
2. Read the corresponding template guide in `skills/<template-name>/SKILL.md`.
3. Follow the layout blueprints, typography scales, and CSS/HTML scaffolding provided in the template references.
